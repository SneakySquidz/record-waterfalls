# Record Waterfalls

This repo is public: keep everything here free of personal and client details.

## Rules for the page
- **Every figure needs a linked source on the page**, under the waterfall it belongs to.
- **Where sources disagree, say so** in the claim box; don't pick one silently.
- **Never fill a gap with an estimate.** A missing figure is shown as "no sourced figure".
- **Each "In the animation" note says what is to scale and what is drawn by eye.** Update the note whenever a scene changes.
- One self-contained `index.html`, no build step. It must open straight from disk with no console errors and all 12 waterfalls selectable.
- When figures are re-checked, update the "Figures checked" line on the page and in the README.

## Report back (every session)
When a session here changes this project's status, next step or a deadline, or finishes something, end your reply with a short block starting `→ CHIEF` (project, what changed, next step, any date) for the owner to paste into his own notes. Generic wording, no names or amounts.

## Working with the owner
- **Ask when in doubt.** If a choice is his, or a request could mean two things, ask rather than guess. Routine, reversible work goes ahead without asking.
- **Questions as a questionnaire:** a short question, then 2–4 options, the recommended one first marked "(Recommended)", each with a one-line reason.
- **Tone:** plain and direct, lead with the answer, no flattery, and say so when a plan looks wrong.
